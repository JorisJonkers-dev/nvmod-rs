# A generated Mod wins over every other Mod in the Staging tree

- **Question:** where does a generated far view (the Toolbox's LOD Mod, marked `[raulGeneratedLod] generated=true` in its `meta.ini`) sit in the Staging tree when other Mods ship files at its paths?
- **Decision:** the Staging plan adds the enabled generated Mods after every other enabled Mod (their order among themselves follows the list), and Overwrite after them. When a generated Mod has archives (`*.bsa`) at its top, loose files of the base copy and of other Mods at the paths those archives hold are left out of the tree, so the archive's file is the one the game reads. An archive that cannot be read is reported as `stage.generated-archive-unreadable` (warning) and leaves the loose files in.
- **Why:** a far view is built for one Load order and its parts belong together: the object blocks keep their UVs into the atlas generated with them. A texture pack that ships its own far-view atlas and far-land tiles (`textures/landscape/lod/<world>/...`) and sits above the LOD Mod replaced the generated atlas, and far rocks and trees were drawn with crate and vehicle textures (nv-rs #834). Ordering by the list alone cannot prevent it, since any Mod installed later goes on top; and a packed far view loses to every loose file whatever the list says.
- **Revisit:** the conflicts report still leaves generated Mods out and does not say which loose files gave way; add that if a user needs to see it.
